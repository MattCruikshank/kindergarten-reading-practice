Kindergarten Reading Practice

At the end of the year, our district administers literacy foundational skill assessments. While not explicitly on the report card, these assessments test your child's fluency and accuracy which are good indicators for future reading and writing success. We want students to know these foundational skills correctly (accuracy), but we also want them to be able to quickly retrieve the information (fluency).

All kindergarten students are assessed on the following skills:

Nonsense Words (2-3 sound words) — accurately read 12 words in 1 minute (i.e. og, fim, dov)

Letter Sounds — accurately identify 42 sounds in 1 minute

High Frequency Words — accurately identify 13 words in 1 minute

Segmenting Words — accurately identify 30 sounds within 3-sound words and 4-sound words (not timed within 1 minute)

The quicker students are able to recall these foundational skills, the more easily they can decode words, make meaning of what they are reading, and build their confidence.
